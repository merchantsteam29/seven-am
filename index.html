Before you wake up

This is a horror game. You will die many times, in ways that are sudden and violent. It deals with drunk driving and a death on the road, and it has jump scares.

How much of each death you see is up to you: Settings → Gore. "Quick cut" (the default) cuts to black at the moment it happens. "Full" shows what is left.

You need to catch the 7:40 bus to work. Something will kill you before then. When it does, you wake up at 7:00 again, and you remember. Use what you learned: every death can be avoided.

Every morning you survive a little longer, the world gets a little less right. Look around. Something happened last night that you don't remember. Not yet.
